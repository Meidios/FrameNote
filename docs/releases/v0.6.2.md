# FrameNote 0.6.2

中文：安装后，视频文件的「打开方式」新增 **FrameNote Player**。选择它会直接播放原片，点击「进入批注」可切换到审阅。选择 **FrameNote** 则沿用原有审阅流程。安装不会更改视频的当前默认应用。视频审阅的音量控制统一在底部播放栏。

English: Choose **FrameNote Player** in Windows Open with to play the original video. Choose **进入批注** to switch to review, or select **FrameNote** to use the existing review workflow. Setup does not change your current default video application. Video review volume controls are now on the bottom transport only.
