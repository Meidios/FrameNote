# FrameNote 0.9.1

- 选中片段后，可以将它贴到同轨上一片段的结尾或下一片段的开头。首段可贴到零点，末段可贴到时间线末尾。这两个操作的快捷键可在「文件 → 设置 → 快捷键 → 时间线」中自定义。
- 拖动片段或批注块时可以吸附到播放指针。裁剪片段时，保留区域的缩略图和波形维持原有时间比例。开启联动后，拖动视频会同步预览原声的移动和裁剪结果。
- 修复同一帧有多条批注时，点击画面编号偶尔无法选中对应批注的问题。

**English**

- Align a selected clip with the end of the previous clip or the start of the next clip on its track. The first clip can snap to zero and the last to the timeline end. Assign shortcuts under File → Settings → Shortcuts → Timeline.
- Snap clips and annotations to the playhead while dragging. Trimming preserves the time scale of thumbnails and waveforms. Linked audio follows the video during drag and trim previews.
- Fixed intermittent selection of the wrong annotation when clicking a numbered badge on a frame with multiple annotations.
