图片和剪贴板截图可直接添加、编辑文字、箭头、框选和图片批注。编号与颜色在画面和右侧列表中对应，缩放、拖动画面时标记跟随原图。文件图片的批注自动保存为本地草稿；导出 .framenote 可一起交付原图和可编辑批注。动图上的批注作用于整张动图。

看图时不显示音量控件，切回视频时恢复上次音量。

**English**

Add and edit text, arrows, rectangles and image annotations on images and clipboard screenshots. Numbers and colors match the canvas and list. Annotations stay attached when panning or zooming. This version saved local drafts for file-based images. Export a .framenote package to share the original image and editable annotations. Annotations on animated images apply to the entire animation.

Image viewing hides the volume controls. Switching back to video restores the previous volume.
