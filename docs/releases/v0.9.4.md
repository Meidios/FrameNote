# FrameNote 0.9.4

- 时间线支持拖动视频片段换序。联动裁剪后拉回片段边缘可恢复批注，拖动或裁剪语音批注时，其标签跟随语音片段，视频和原声轨保持原位。
- 标尺支持拖拽或两点测量，可设置默认单位并按帧标定比例。绘制角度时可实时查看度数，角度和标尺的默认批注文字包含读数。放大镜的取景框与预览框可分别移动。
- 录制面板会记住捕获来源和音频设备，可选择自动保存录制副本。通用设置可指定导出目录，Excel 可选导出列及 ZIP 打包，MP4 可显示带批注位置的进度条。导出窗口整合了进度、完成提示、打开目录和复制操作。
- 设置增加英文界面、快捷键查找、分模块使用说明和问题反馈入口。恢复默认值仅作用于当前设置页。
- 小窗口可展开捕获侧栏，从「更多剪辑工具」访问片段贴齐操作。批注编号按底色选用黑字或白字。「按时间编号」按起点重新编号，保留批注内容、颜色和附件。
- 改善 Player 首帧加载、图片导航图标的可见度，以及键盘激活按钮和选择同帧批注时的焦点处理。修复主界面和 Player 从全屏返回后标题栏图标与点击区域错位的问题。

**English**

- Drag video clips to reorder them. Extending a linked clip after trimming restores its annotations. Moving or trimming a voice note keeps its label in sync and leaves the video and original audio in place.
- Measure by dragging a ruler or clicking two endpoints. Choose a default unit and calibrate the scale for a frame. Angles update as you draw, and new ruler and angle notes include the measured value. Move a magnifier’s source and preview independently.
- Capture sources and audio devices are remembered between sessions. Recordings can keep an automatic copy. Set a default export folder, choose Excel columns and ZIP packaging, or include an annotation progress bar in MP4. The export window shows progress and offers actions to open the folder or copy the result.
- Settings now include an English interface, shortcut search, a guide organized by workflow and a feedback page. Restoring defaults affects the current settings page.
- In small windows, open capture controls from the header and find clip alignment under More editing tools. Annotation badges use black or white text according to their background. Renumber by time preserves note content, colors and attachments.
- Improved first-frame loading in Player, visibility of image navigation controls and keyboard focus when activating buttons or selecting notes on the same frame. Fixed title bar icons shifting away from their hit targets after leaving fullscreen in the editor and Player.
