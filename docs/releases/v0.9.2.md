# FrameNote 0.9.2

- 剪短视频后，导出的 MP4 在最后一段有效内容结束时停止。片段之间的留白、较晚结束的原声、语音和批注都会保留。
- 批注列表右上角新增「重新排序」，可以按起始时间从 1 连续编号。同一起点的批注保持原有先后顺序，支持撤销。
- MP4 和 Excel 导出改用和导入一致的居中百分比进度条，下方可取消导出。Excel 导出按钮改为带向上箭头的宽表格图标。
- 图片查看器和视频 Player 的播放列表入口移到查看区左上角，以半透明图标显示。点击图标或按 Alt+L 可平滑展开和收起，右侧画面同步调整宽度。

**English**

- After trimming a video, MP4 export stops at the end of the last remaining content. Gaps between clips and later audio, voice recordings or annotations are preserved.
- Renumber annotations from 1 in start-time order using Renumber above the list. Ties keep their order, and the operation supports undo.
- MP4 and Excel exports use the same centered percentage progress bar as imports, with Cancel below it. The Excel button now shows a wide table with an export arrow.
- The image viewer and video Player now have a translucent playlist icon at the upper left. Click it or press Alt+L to animate the playlist open or closed while resizing the canvas.
