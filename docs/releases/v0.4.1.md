在图片审阅侧栏点击「复制批注图」即可粘贴到聊天框；「另存为 PNG」可保存到本地。两种方式均按原图分辨率输出完整图片，保留编号、颜色、箭头、框和附图，不受窗口大小、缩放或拖动位置影响。动图导出为静态首帧。要继续编辑批注或保留动画，请使用「导出审阅包」。

**English**

Choose Copy annotated image in the image review sidebar to paste the result into a chat, or Save as PNG to keep a local copy. Both render the full image at its original resolution, including numbers, colors, arrows, rectangles and attachments, regardless of window size, zoom or pan. Animated images export as a still of the first frame. Export a review package to preserve animation or continue editing annotations.
