# FrameNote 0.9.6

- 发送到 REAPER 时，剪辑后的视频、录制语音、导入的音频和透明批注图会作为独立 Item 放到同一轨道。视频在最后一个视频片段结束处停止，以视频文件名命名的总 Region 覆盖全部导入内容。点批注图使用图片源的完整长度，区间批注图循环至区间末尾。
- REAPER 确认接收后，发送进度窗会自动关闭。在「设置 → REAPER集成」可查看脚本状态、自动识别或手动选择安装位置，以及安装、更新和卸载脚本。
- Excel 的 Region 默认导出 GIF。与录制语音或导入音频批注重叠的 Region 导出带声音的 MP4。普通点批注导出 PNG，带声音的点批注可导出 MP4。工作簿同时链接录音和导入的原始音频文件。
- 拖入音频时，批注轨会预览放置位置和片段长度。录音时会实时绘制波形，已导入的音频片段也会显示波形。修复开启 Link 后删除音频批注会连带删除视频和原声的问题。
- 加快大视频进入审阅画面的速度，缩略图和波形在后台加载。优化 MP4、GIF、Excel 和 REAPER 的导出速度，支持的 NVIDIA 显卡可通过 NVENC 加速 H.264 视频导出。
- 批注列表中的「画面编号」开关可记住显示偏好。「距离标尺」移至「设置 → 批注工具」。拖动排序工具和预设颜色时可预览插入位置，修正了部分工具图标、帮助菜单跳转，以及混合播放列表中首张图片的缩略图。

**English**

- Send the edited video, recorded voice notes, imported audio and transparent annotation overlays to one REAPER track as separate items. The video ends at the last edited picture. A region named after the video spans the entire import. Point overlays use the full still-image source length and range overlays loop to the end of their region.
- The progress window closes when REAPER confirms the import. In Settings → REAPER integration, check the script status, detect or choose the installation folder, and install, update or uninstall the receiver.
- Excel exports regions as GIFs by default. Regions that overlap recorded or imported annotation audio export as MP4s with sound. Ordinary point notes export as PNGs and point notes with audio can export as MP4s. The workbook also links to recordings and original imported audio files.
- Dragging in audio previews the clip position and length. The annotation track displays live waveforms while recording and shows waveforms for imported clips. Deleting annotation audio with Link enabled now preserves the video and original soundtrack.
- Large videos open for review sooner while thumbnails and waveforms load in the background. MP4, GIF, Excel and REAPER exports are faster. H.264 video exports can use NVENC on supported NVIDIA GPUs.
- The on-screen number toggle remembers your preference. Distance ruler settings are now under Annotation tools. Reordering tools and colors previews the insertion position. This release also fixes several tool icons, Help menu navigation and the first image thumbnail in mixed playlists.
