# FrameNote 0.9.5

- 时间线上左键拖动可框选多个片段，一起移动或删除。选中视频片段后，Ctrl+X 剪切、Ctrl+C 复制、Ctrl+V 粘贴到播放头，Ctrl+D 在选区之后重复。拖动剪辑边缘时会预览当前帧，播放到最后一段视频、音频或批注结束时自动停止。
- 右键拖出时间选区，可循环播放或仅导出该范围内的 MP4、GIF。在时间尺上拖动选区两端可微调范围。手动开启循环后按播放键开始，双击 Region 会选中对应时长并立即循环。
- 箭头和线可以连续单击绘制曲线，右键、双击或 Enter 收笔。新增自由画笔。滚轮可调整框选大小、放大镜、曲率或笔宽。悬停在批注上时，Alt+滚轮切换预设颜色，Shift+滚轮调整不透明度。
- 批注轨固定为一行，点击数量标记即可选择重叠批注。点击空白、时间尺或媒体片段可取消批注选择。设置中的「批注工具」支持拖动预设颜色和工具排序，可隐藏工具、添加分隔线或加宽按钮。
- 语音批注仅录制麦克风，录制期间暂时静音系统默认输出，结束后恢复。新录音使用 MP3。音频文件可以附加到已有批注，也可以直接拖入批注音轨。Excel 导出会附带可点击的录音 MP3 和导入音频的原文件，旧 M4A 语音批注也可导出为 MP3。
- 录屏期间默认自动隐藏主窗口，可在设置中关闭。选中 FrameNote 自身窗口录制时仍可录下界面。可拖动悬浮停止按钮，悬停显示暂停和继续。修复悬浮停止后的录制状态显示与空格键焦点，大视频加载时显示进度。

## English

- Drag on a track to select, move or delete multiple clips. Use Ctrl+X/C/V to cut, copy and paste at the playhead, or Ctrl+D to duplicate after the selection. Trimming previews the frame at the edge. Playback stops at the last item on the timeline.
- Right-drag to select a time range for looping or MP4/GIF export. Drag either edge on the ruler to refine it. The loop toggle waits for Play. Double-clicking a Region selects its duration and starts looping.
- Click successive points to draw curved arrows and lines, then right-click, double-click or press Enter to finish. A freehand brush is also available. Use the wheel to resize or reshape the active tool, Alt+wheel to step through preset colors, and Shift+wheel to adjust opacity.
- Timeline annotations share one row. Click the count badge to choose an overlapping note. Reorder preset colors and tools by dragging in Settings, hide tools, add separators or make buttons wider.
- Voice notes record the microphone to MP3 and temporarily mute default system playback. Attach audio files to notes or drop them onto the audio annotation track. Excel exports link to recorded MP3s and original imported files, including MP3 copies of legacy M4A voice notes.
- The main window can hide during screen recording. Drag the floating Stop button to reposition it or hover for Pause and Resume. Selecting FrameNote itself as the capture window keeps it visible. The recording indicator and Space key now return to playback after stopping, and large videos show loading progress.
